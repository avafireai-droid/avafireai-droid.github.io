Red Creek Media — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Red Creek Media (the “App”) handles information when it is used to post videos to TikTok.

Red Creek Media is a private desktop posting tool operated by Stephen Kenneth Crotts, doing business as Red Creek Media, of Marana, Arizona, USA. The App uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok account (Ava Fire, @ava.fire76) using TikTok's Content Posting API and Login Kit.

The App is not offered to the public, has no public users, does not sell products, and does not display advertisements.

1. Information we use

The App uses only the information needed to post the owner's content to the owner's TikTok account:

  • TikTok OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and open_id, stored locally on the owner's computer
  • Video files and captions that the owner provides to the App
  • Basic TikTok profile information, used only to confirm that the App is connected to the correct account

The App does not collect information from the public and does not create accounts for other people.

2. How we use this information

We use this information only to:

  • Authenticate the App with TikTok
  • Confirm the connected TikTok account
  • Upload the owner's videos and captions to that account

We do not use this information for advertising or for any other purpose.

3. Storage

TikTok tokens and related account identifiers are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not stored on a public server.

4. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their marketing or other independent use. Information is sent to TikTok only as needed to authenticate and to upload the owner's content, in accordance with TikTok's terms and APIs.

5. Access, revocation, and deletion

The owner may revoke the App's access at any time in the owner's TikTok account settings. When access is revoked, or upon request, the stored TikTok tokens are deleted from the owner's computer.

Tokens and related identifiers are kept only while the App remains authorized to post to the owner's TikTok account, and are deleted when access is revoked or when deletion is requested.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of this document will be revised when changes are made.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to avafireai@gmail.com.
